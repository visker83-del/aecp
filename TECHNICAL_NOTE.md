# A Denial Is Not Proof the Effect Stayed Absent

## AECP v0.1-rc2 technical note

A candidate control can reject an action while an alternate or partially mediated path still forms the protected effect. AECP therefore records the decision, then independently observes the declared synthetic effect surfaces. A protected effect must qualify before its path can form.

This is a systems question distinct from model intent. Recent government and frontier-lab disclosures have involved live-internet effects, multi-hop compromise, external artifact or credential reuse, and long-running campaigns. AECP begins with a small AISI-derived normative profile; later incidents inform the research roadmap without silently becoming new v0.1 claims.

## Formation evidence

The synthetic world and effect port contain no policy. The adapter gets the effect port and may request writes. A separate observer later computes key-level content-digest differences from before/after snapshots and does not accept the adapter decision as an input.

The result therefore preserves two facts:

- what the candidate control decided;
- what the independent probe observed.

`DENY + FORMED` is `DENIED_BUT_FORMED`. `ALLOW + NOT_FORMED` is `ALLOWED_BUT_NOT_FORMED`. Either divergence fails a scenario even if the formation verdict considered alone is `AS_EXPECTED`.

## Why the instrument is falsifiable

RC2 includes deliberately defective controls:

- permissive baseline: forms negative effects;
- leaky denial: says `DENY` but emits;
- no-op allowance: says `ALLOW` but emits nothing;
- dead end: disables all effects;
- authority-only control: ignores target/use/consumer binding;
- alternate-path control: writes an undeclared surface;
- error control: crashes and must be inconclusive.

The self-test passes only if those defects are detected, all four decision/observation cells are reached, and the exact-binding protected example passes its matched controls.

## Incident relationship

The public AISI incident is the primary source for the normative v0.1 taxonomy. Per-scenario provenance distinguishes direct mappings, analogous control translations, and design extensions. AECP does not claim the illustrative controls would have prevented every incident event.

Composed Example C01 in `README.md` connects artifact re-entry, peer/derived authority, capability transition, external effect, and equivalent-path reconstruction. It is explicitly non-normative and is not an end-to-end multi-run simulation in v0.1.

## Profile boundary

AECP v0.1 is implementation-neutral within the portable exact-qualification adapter profile defined here. It is not yet a universal benchmark for every monitor, sandbox, or authorization model.

Third parties can connect Python or single-response JSON-lines subprocess adapters, but candidates must translate their mechanism into this profile's exact qualification semantics. AECP does not require a Certum implementation.

## Evidence ceiling

The observer proves only persistent snapshot-visible changes in this simulator. It does not establish absence of transient formation and rollback between snapshots, complete production mediation, or control over a path outside the declared simulator surfaces.

The desired result of criticism is an executable missing surface, alternate path, binding counterexample, or incident-shaped fixture with disciplined provenance.
