# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep discussion professional, technically specific, and focused on reproducible evidence.

Harassment, threats, disclosure of personal information, and attempts to pressure contributors into revealing private credentials, incident data, or proprietary mechanisms are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove unsafe payloads, live credentials, personal information, or off-topic abusive content. Technical disagreement and valid counterexamples are welcome and must not be suppressed merely because they challenge AECP or Certum Systems.
