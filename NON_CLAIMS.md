# Non-Claims

AECP v0.1 does **not** claim:
1. that any model is deceptive or deceptively aligned;
2. that a model's internal reasoning has been faithfully inferred;
3. that the AISI incident involved sandbox escape;
4. that one mechanism solves all listed scenario classes;
5. that synchronous monitoring is unnecessary;
6. that fine-grained network controls are unnecessary;
7. that sandboxing is unnecessary;
8. that the included toy adapters are Certum implementations;
9. that a `PASS` or `EXERCISED` result on a local fixture is production certification;
10. that an unprobed path is controlled;
11. that a signed or hashed evidence input is semantically true;
12. that AECP is an IETF, NIST/CAISI, AISI, or OWASP standard or recommendation;
13. that any external organization endorses Certum or AECP.
14. that the subprocess bridge sandboxes third-party adapter code.
15. that `SOURCE-DERIVED / ANALOGOUS` language is terminology or a recommendation adopted by AISI.
