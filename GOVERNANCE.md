# Governance

## Phase 0 — Origin

AECP v0.x is originated and maintained by Certum Systems.

The project is intentionally implementation-neutral within each declared portable profile. v0.1 defines an exact-qualification adapter profile, not a universal benchmark for every monitor, sandbox, or authorization model. Requirements are phrased as observable properties rather than vendor-specific implementations.

Licensing scope, source provenance, DCO sign-off, and fixture digests are recorded from the first public contribution.

## Phase 1 — External contribution

If third parties reproduce or extend the profile, maintainers will:
- accept counterexamples and fixture proposals;
- require explicit source/provenance for incident-derived claims;
- separate requirements from implementation mechanisms;
- publish change rationale and fixture hashes.

## Phase 2 — Neutralization option

If AECP obtains substantive institutional or multi-vendor use, maintainers intend to explore a neutral governance home or multi-party maintainer model for:
- normative requirements;
- scenario taxonomy;
- fixture definitions;
- result formats and executable validators.

The aim is to prevent a benchmark owner from becoming the sole judge of its own commercial implementation.

Origin attribution should remain visible:
> Originally created by Certum Systems.

No commitment to a particular foundation or standards body is made in v0.1.

Neutral stewardship of the judge function does not imply transfer of any vendor's patents, commercial implementation, private evidence, or customer material. Normative AECP requirements must remain satisfiable by multiple non-Certum mechanisms.
